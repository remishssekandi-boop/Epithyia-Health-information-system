# Kampala hospital directory

The directory starts with six Kampala hospitals. Their contact details and services were checked against hospital websites and Kampala Capital City Authority (KCCA) listings. Details for the first five hospitals were checked on **18 November 2026**; Nakasero Hospital was checked on **26 November 2026**. Administrators can add additional entries; those are marked unverified until their details are checked independently. Published sources do not guarantee that a service, number, or schedule is unchanged or available at the time of a visit. Call ahead when possible. This directory is not an emergency response service.

## Mengo Hospital

- **Address:** Sir Albert Cook Road, Mengo, Kampala; P.O. Box 7161.
- **Telephone:** +256 771 370 263; +256 771 370 264; +256 757 143 951; +256 757 143 961; +256 312 307 100.
- **Hours:** The Accident and Emergency department is advertised as 24/7. Clinic hours vary.
- **Emergency:** No separate emergency number is published. Call the hospital and ask for Accident and Emergency.
- **Published services:** Internal medicine, surgery, eye care, obstetrics and gynecology, paediatrics, dental, endocrinology and NCDs, pathology, outpatient care, public health and research, laboratory, mental health, Accident and Emergency, intensive care, ambulance, blood bank, and urology.
- **Sources:** [Hospital contact page](https://mengohospital.org/contact-us/), [hospital services](https://mengohospital.org/services/), [A&E announcement](https://mengohospital.org/mengo-hospital-expands-accident-and-emergency-department/), [KCCA listing](https://visitkampala.kcca.go.ug/business_details/mengo-hospital).

## Rubaga (Lubaga) Hospital

- **Address:** Next to Lubaga Cathedral, Lubaga Hill, Kampala; P.O. Box 14130.
- **Telephone:** +256 200 244 800.
- **Hours:** The hospital contact page advertises 24/7 service; KCCA lists the emergency department as 24/7. Clinic schedules may vary.
- **Emergency:** No separate emergency number is published. Call the main hospital number and ask for the emergency department.
- **Published services:** Cardiology, surgery, dialysis and transplant care, CT/MRI imaging, intensive care, maternity, paediatrics, ENT, neurosurgery, dental, and emergency care.
- **Sources:** [Hospital contact page](https://lubagahospital.org/contact-us/), [KCCA listing](https://visitkampala.kcca.go.ug/business_details/lubaga-hospital).

## Old Kampala Hospital

- **Address:** Old Kampala Hill, opposite Old Kampala Police Post.
- **Telephone:** +256 752 024 333.
- **Hours:** The hospital advertises 24/7 service and a 24-hour Accident and Emergency unit. Clinic hours vary.
- **Emergency:** +256 752 024 333 is published by the hospital for emergency care.
- **Published services:** Diagnostics and therapeutics, eye care, dental care, primary care and medicine, obstetrics and gynecology, surgery, and neurosurgery. Confirm department availability with the hospital.
- **Sources:** [Hospital contact page](https://oldkampalahospital.org/contact/), [hospital homepage](https://oldkampalahospital.org/).

## Kibuli Muslim Hospital

- **Address:** Kibuli Hill, Makindye Division, off Prince Badru Kakungulu Road, Kampala; P.O. Box 24548.
- **Telephone:** +256 414 236 476/7; +256 702 542 719.
- **Hours:** KCCA lists emergency services as 24/7. General and outpatient clinic hours were not published in the checked sources.
- **Emergency:** +256 414 236 476/7 is labelled “Emergency Call” by the hospital.
- **Published services:** Infectious disease/HIV clinic, pharmacy, paediatrics, eye centre, surgery and theatre, radiology, physiotherapy, dental clinic, laboratory, emergency, inpatient and outpatient care, maternity, and dialysis.
- **Sources:** [Hospital contact page](https://kibulimuslimhospital.ug/contact/), [hospital services](https://kibulimuslimhospital.ug/services/), [KCCA listing](https://visitkampala.kcca.go.ug/business_details/kibuli-hospital).

## Mulago National Referral Hospital

- **Address:** Mulago Hill, Kawempe North Division, Kampala; P.O. Box 7051.
- **Telephone:** +256 414 554 008; +256 414 554 001.
- **Hours:** Medical Emergency, surgical Accident and Emergency, and paediatric Acute Care operate 24/7. Assessment-centre walk-in clinics run during regular working hours; specialist schedules vary.
- **Emergency:** Medical Emergency: +256 414 675 065. Acute Care Unit: +256 414 675 066.
- **Published services:** Paediatrics, surgery, internal medicine, radiology, clinical laboratory, pathology, nuclear medicine, ENT, neurology, rheumatology, renal dialysis, orthopedics, obstetrics, gynecology, neonatology, and emergency services.
- **Sources:** [hospital homepage](https://mulagohospital.go.ug/), [hospital emergency contacts](https://mulagohospital.go.ug/contact2.html), [KCCA listing](https://visitkampala.kcca.go.ug/business_details/mulago-national-referral-hospital).

## Nakasero Hospital

- **Address:** 14A Akii Bua Road, Nakasero, Kampala.
- **Telephone:** +256 312 531 400; +256 800 200 035; +256 776 970 220.
- **Hours:** The hospital describes 24/7 emergency support. Call ahead to confirm outpatient clinic schedules and service availability.
- **Emergency:** Call the hospital and ask for the emergency department.
- **Published services:** Outpatient and inpatient care, emergency and critical care, surgery, maternity and neonatal care, diagnostics and imaging, laboratory, pharmacy, specialist care, and physiotherapy/rehabilitation.
- **Sources:** [hospital contact page](https://nakaserohospital.com/contact-us/), [hospital patient support and services](https://nakaserohospital.com/patient-support/), [KCCA listing and directions](https://visitkampala.kcca.go.ug/business_details/nakasero-hospital).
